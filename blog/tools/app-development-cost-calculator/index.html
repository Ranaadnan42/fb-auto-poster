How app development cost is estimated

Developers price apps by time: the hours needed for each screen and feature, multiplied by an hourly rate. This calculator uses typical hour ranges per feature, then adjusts for design level and the number of platforms.

  • Platforms: one cross-platform codebase (Flutter or React Native) usually costs about 30% more than a single platform, while two separate native apps can cost close to double.
  • Features: login, payments, chat and admin panels each need screens, a server side and testing. Chat and ordering are among the biggest.
  • Design: custom branding and animations take more design and front-end time than a template.
  • Team location: the same app can cost three to four times more with a US or UK agency than with an experienced offshore team.

Costs after launch

Plan roughly 15-20% of the build cost per year for updates, new phone OS versions, bug fixes and server costs. Apps that are never updated start breaking within a year or two as iOS and Android change.

Does a small business need an app or just a website?

If customers order or book from you often (restaurants, salons, gyms, repeat retail), an app with loyalty rewards and push notifications can pay for itself. If most visitors come once, a fast mobile-friendly website is usually the better first investment.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to build an app for a small business?

A simple app with a few features often costs about $5,000-$25,000 with a freelancer or small studio, and $40,000-$150,000 or more with a US or UK agency. The feature list and platforms matter more than anything else.

How long does it take to build an app?

A focused first version usually takes about 6-16 weeks. Apps with ordering, payments, chat and admin panels take longer, especially if they must connect to an existing POS or inventory system.

Is cross-platform cheaper than native?

Usually yes. One cross-platform codebase serves iOS and Android and typically costs much less than building two native apps, with good performance for most business apps.

Are these app cost estimates exact?

No. They are planning ranges based on typical hours and rates. A real quote depends on your exact screens, integrations and the team you choose.
