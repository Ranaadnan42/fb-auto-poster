What decides the cost of a small business website?

Website prices range from almost nothing to tens of thousands of dollars because "a website" can mean very different jobs. Four things move the price the most:

  • Who builds it. A website builder (Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and similar) is cheapest in money but costs your time. Freelancers sit in the middle; agencies charge more for strategy, design and project management.
  • The type of site. A five-page brochure site is far simpler than an online store with payments, shipping rules and stock levels.
  • Content. Writing text, taking photos and creating a logo are often bigger jobs than the build itself.
  • Features. Booking, payments, multiple languages and chat each add setup and testing time.

One-time build vs yearly running costs

Every website has running costs after launch: the domain name, hosting or a builder subscription, security updates and small edits. Budget for them from the start - a cheap build with expensive monthly add-ons can cost more over three years than a fixed-price custom site.

Questions to ask before you hire someone

  • Will the domain and hosting be registered in my name?
  • Can I edit text and photos myself after launch?
  • Is basic SEO (titles, descriptions, speed, mobile layout) included?
  • What does a small change cost after the project ends?

Frequently asked questions

How much does a small business website cost in 2026?

Roughly $100-$600 a year if you build it yourself with a website builder, about $1,000-$5,000 with a freelancer, and $5,000-$15,000 or more with an agency. Online stores and booking sites cost more.

What are the yearly costs of running a website?

Usually a domain name at about $10-$20 a year plus hosting or a builder plan, which can range from about $60 to $600 a year. Online stores often pay extra for apps, themes and payment features.

Is a website builder good enough for a small business?

For many service businesses, yes. Builders are fast and affordable. A custom site makes more sense when you need special features, many languages, tight integration with your POS or better long-term control.

Are these website prices exact?

No. They are typical ranges to help you plan and compare quotes. Prices change by country, provider and scope, so use the result as a starting point for discussions.
