Web Builder Auto Publisher is a private tool used by Muhammad Adnan (Web Builder) to publish his own original short videos to his own social media accounts, including the pages Islamic Motivational Videos and Hansi Ka Adda.

Each day the tool creates short videos (Islamic motivation and family-friendly comedy), and after the account owner has authorized it once with TikTok Login Kit, it uploads those videos to the owner's TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API. The tool is not offered to the public and only works with accounts owned by the developer.
