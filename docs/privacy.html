Web Builder Auto Publisher ("the Service") respects your privacy.

What we collect

When an account owner authorizes the Service with TikTok, we receive an access token and refresh token and basic profile information (open id, display name, avatar) through TikTok Login Kit. We do not collect any other personal data, and we do not collect data from viewers.

When an account owner authorizes the Service with Pinterest, we receive an access token and refresh token through Pinterest OAuth, used only to create boards and Pins on the owner's own Pinterest account.

How we use it

Tokens are used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never sold or shared with third parties.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions), and in Pinterest under Settings > Security > Apps. To request deletion of stored tokens, email ranaadnan320@gmail.com.
